FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with micro-influencers?

Yes. Selection is based on audience relevance, credibility, content quality and expected impact—not follower count alone.

Can UGC be used in ads?

Yes, when paid usage rights and platform permissions are agreed before production.

How do you measure influencer campaigns?

Measurement can include qualified reach, engagement, content quality, traffic, leads, sales and paid amplification performance.

Which platforms do you support?

Programs can include Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook, X, podcasts, blogs and other relevant creator channels.
