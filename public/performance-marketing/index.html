FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which advertising platforms do you manage?

Prime Polo supports Google, Meta, LinkedIn, display, video and retargeting based on business fit.

Do you guarantee ROAS?

No responsible agency can guarantee a fixed return. Performance depends on demand, offer, margin, creative, competition, sales follow-up and tracking quality.

Can you fix conversion tracking?

Yes. Scope can include analytics, conversion events, campaign parameters and CRM handoff planning.

Is media spend included in your fee?

Media and service fees are scoped separately or together depending on the engagement. The proposal makes the structure explicit.
