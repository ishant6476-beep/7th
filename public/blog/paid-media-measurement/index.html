How to Measure Paid Media More Honestly

Platform dashboards are useful diagnostic tools, but commercial decisions require a wider view of attribution, lead quality and economics.

Published 16 August 20268 minute readPrime Polo Editorial
Start a conversationCall 7903946440

Platform dashboards are useful diagnostic tools, but commercial decisions require a wider view of attribution, lead quality and economics.

Build a metric hierarchy

Begin with profitable revenue, qualified pipeline or another business outcome. Use CAC, conversion rate and lead quality as management metrics. Treat CPM, clicks and engagement as signals that help explain performance—not final proof of value.

Connect systems

Preserve campaign parameters, define conversion events, validate analytics and connect leads to CRM outcomes. When sales happen offline, feed qualified and closed outcomes back into reporting instead of optimizing only for form submissions.

Use tests that can teach

Write the hypothesis, variable, audience, offer and decision rule before launch. Avoid changing audience, creative, landing page and bidding simultaneously when the purpose is learning.

Review incrementality

Ask what would have happened without the campaign. Use geographic tests, holdouts, brand-search trends and blended economics when appropriate. No single attribution model is perfect, so decisions should combine evidence.
