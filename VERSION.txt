Prime Polo recovery build: 6.0-segmented-intake

This version:
- Adds Influencer, Company and Agency profile options
- Collects required email and phone/WhatsApp details
- Uses role-specific occupation and business questionnaires
- Lists the complete Prime Polo service catalog for multi-selection
- Saves validated submissions to a private Supabase marketing_intakes table
- Includes consent, anti-spam protection and endpoint rate limiting
- Keeps Groq AI, Gemini fallback and the built-in knowledge base

Required setup:
1. Run supabase/marketing_intakes.sql in Supabase SQL Editor
2. Add SUPABASE_URL and SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY in Vercel
3. Redeploy
