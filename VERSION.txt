Prime Polo recovery build: 8.0-dashboard-work-profiles

New authenticated dashboard onboarding:
- Company
- Influencer
- Other

Company profiles collect company, role, industry, business model, size, website,
location, markets, products/services, customers, marketing activity, goals and challenges.

Influencer profiles collect creator name, username, platforms (YouTube, Instagram
and others), channel/profile links, website, niche, audience, reach, languages,
formats, experience, collaborations, goals and challenges.

Other profiles collect occupation, organization, work category/description,
website, portfolio/social links, experience, location/markets, audience/customers,
goals, challenges and additional free-form details.

Every type also collects full name, authenticated email, required phone/WhatsApp,
location, preferred contact method and services of interest.

Required database step:
Run supabase/user_work_profiles.sql once in Supabase SQL Editor.

Profiles are stored in public.user_work_profiles and linked to auth.users with
ON DELETE CASCADE.
